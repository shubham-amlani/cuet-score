⚖️ Disclaimer & Privacy

This application is a non-commercial hobby project created solely for the convenience of students. It is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by the National Testing Agency (NTA), Digialm, or any governmental body.

Data Usage: By using this tool, you acknowledge that your Candidate Name and Total Score will be stored in our database to generate real-time platform rankings and percentiles. We do not extract or store sensitive personal information such as Date of Birth, Category, or contact details.
